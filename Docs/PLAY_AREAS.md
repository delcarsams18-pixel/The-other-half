# THE OTHER HALF — Play Areas (Benway, Missouri — 2526)

Setting: 500 years from now. Failed policies (2025-2070) → every country bankrupt (2070-2120) → The Long Static (2120-2400, no nations, only zones) → Benway, Missouri emerges from rubble (2400-2526). Map = all of Missouri reimagined, fictional. Bigger than GTA.

## District 1: Home Base District 7
- **Theme:** Pulse Plait Tech HQ, safe zone, neon-blue corporate
- **Enemies:** None (tutorial zone)
- **Objective:** Learn movement, shooting, meet squad
- **Story:** Lonzo gears up, Operation Recover briefing

## District 2: Prison District (Jeff City ruins)
- **Boss:** The Warden
- **Theme:** Concrete ruins, searchlights, cell blocks, white/black security armor
- **Enemies:** Prison Sec troopers, riot drones
- **Objective:** Breach the prison, defeat Warden, free informant

## District 3: Red Light Row (St Louis river neon)
- **Boss:** Neon Queen
- **Theme:** Pink/magenta neon, riverfront clubs, holographic ads
- **Enemies:** Neon enforcers, dazzle drones
- **Objective:** Shut down signal relays, confront Neon Queen

## District 4: Scrapyard (KC yards)
- **Boss:** Rust Father
- **Theme:** Rust, gears, steam, junk golems
- **Enemies:** Scrap bots, rust hounds
- **Objective:** Destroy scrap forges, defeat Rust Father

## District 5: Herb Fields (Columbia agri-labs)
- **Boss:** The Chemist
- **Theme:** Greenhouses, agri-labs, toxic fog pockets
- **Enemies:** Chem troopers, toxic crawlers
- **Objective:** Sabotage corrupt labs, defeat Chemist, secure herb samples (BZ)

## District 6: Kennel Row
- **Boss:** Hound Master (Darrel)
- **Theme:** Industrial kennels, cages, red emergency lighting
- **Enemies:** Robotic pit bulls, kennel guards
- **Darrel Law:** BIG pit bulls = KEEPERS (special models with Lonzo tech, 2 as bodyguards). SMALL = SELL (old models, no match).
- **Objective:** Free captive animals, defeat Hound Master

## District 7: Blade Alley
- **Boss:** Blade Mother
- **Theme:** Narrow alleys, neon blades, white/pink accents
- **Enemies:** Blade cultists, edge drones
- **Objective:** Survive the gauntlet, defeat Blade Mother

## Easter Plains
- **Theme:** Open plains, scattered settlements
- **Role:** Travel/connector zone, side missions

## Signal Tower (Archivist)
- **Boss:** The Archivist / Signal Breaker (sub-boss)
- **Theme:** Massive comm tower, data streams
- **Objective:** Cut the signal network before the Citadel

## Final: Overlord Citadel
- **Boss:** The Overlord
- **Theme:** Black fortress, red core, massive scale
- **Enemies:** Elite guard, all underling types
- **Objective:** Reach Carrie, break brainwashing, final confrontation
- **Story:** Sentimental reunion — Carrie remembers through the control
- **Carrie Lock:** RED ARMOR RED HELMET BLUE CORE BLUE EYES. Do not alter.
