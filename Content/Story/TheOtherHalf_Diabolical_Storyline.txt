THE OTHER HALF - PULSE PLAIT - BENWAY CITY 1000KM
Motto: Villains: DOMINATE - ENSLAVE - CORRUPT / Heroes: SURVIVE - CONTROL - TRANSCEND

LORE DROP - The Diabolical Twist:
Benway City isn't a city. It's a BODY. 1000km of living concrete that feeds on people's OTHER HALF.
Every person in Benway is born with 2 halves - their body, and their pulse. The Overlord built the city to STEAL the pulse half.

Operation: GET HER BACK / OPERATION RECOVER is the same op - Carrie is the key. Her cyber-psionic brain is the only one that can hold BOTH halves without dying.

ACT 1 - SURVIVE / DOMINATE - 0-300km
District: Prison + Red Light + Scrapyard
Bosses: THE WARDEN + NEON QUEEN + RUST FATHER

Lonzo (Tech Specialist) wakes up - his tech arm is his dead brother's arm grafted on. Carrie, his other half, has been taken. Her pulse is being used to power Benway Core.

The Warden runs Prison District - he doesn't lock people in, he locks their OTHER HALF out. Neon Queen runs Red Light - she doesn't sell bodies, she sells your other half for 10 minutes of feeling whole. Rust Father builds a wall from the scrap of failed halves - 150km of screaming metal.

Darrel (Kennel Boss) joins - his dogs can SMELL your other half. That's how you find Carrie.

ACT 2 - ENSLAVE / CONTROL - 300-700km
District: Corrupt Herb Lab + Kennel District + Blade District
Bosses: THE CHEMIST + HOUND MASTER + BLADE MOTHER

The Chemist (Corrupt Herb Lab) - BZ's old partner. BZ = Herbs Specialist. Chemist found how to smoke your other half. BZ's green herbs let you SEE your other half for 5 mins. Chemist's version lets The Overlord CONTROL it.

Hound Master - Deacon's brother. Deacon is Blade Expert. Hound Master's robot dogs don't bite your body - they bite your pulse and drag it back to Overlord's cage. Father-in-Law runs Bunny Coins - not money. Each bunny coin is a trapped kid's other half. That's the currency of Benway. You pay with your soul.

You need all 7 hero roster to breach core: Lonzo to hack, Carrie to psionic link, Darrel to sniff, Deacon to cut, Father-in-Law to bribe with bunny coins, BZ to make you see, Big Nate...

ACT 3 - CORRUPT / TRANSCEND - 700-1000km - BENWAY CORE
Boss: THE OVERLORD - Final Boss

The Overlord isn't a man. He's the first person who gave up BOTH halves willingly. He's a cage with your other half inside. Every citizen's missing half is in that cage in the poster - blue lightning.

Twist: BIG NATE - Easter Egg - sitting on bench in pink shorts with basket of eggs. He's not Easter Egg - he's THE ORIGINAL. He laid the first Benway egg. Inside each egg is a new city. Benway City - MO sign in your poster is LIE - it's not Missouri, it's Mother Organism.

Final fight: You don't kill Overlord. You have to give him YOUR other half. Lonzo has to cut off his tech arm - which is actually Carrie's pulse arm - and put it in the cage. Carrie transcends - she becomes both halves.

Ending: If you SURVIVE + CONTROL, you TRANSCEND - you get her back but Benway City wakes up and it's 1000km bigger now - bigger than GTA because it grew.

CREDITS STINGER: Big Nate winks, opens basket, 1000 blue eggs pulse.
