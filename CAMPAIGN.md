# THE OTHER HALF — Full Campaign Canvas

## Structure: OPEN WORLD
Benway, Missouri is one continuous open world — all districts connected, free to explore. Missions unlock in story order but the player can roam anywhere. Districts stream in as the player travels.
Benway, Missouri, 2526 — 500 years after collapse. Lonzo owns Pulse Plet Technologies. After an accident, half of Carrie's brain was replaced with robotics/AI — she kept her memories and gained telekinesis, mind-reading, and pulse blasts. Beezy (herb expert) was drugged and tricked into stealing a hard drive carrying Carrie's brain update. The villains used it to brainwash Carrie and built an army from Lonzo's stolen tech. Now Lonzo fights through Benway City to get her back.

## Act 1: Sparks in the Static
**District 7 — Home Base (Tutorial)**
Lonzo gears up at Pulse Plet HQ. P-Mac briefs him: Carrie's signal is moving through the districts, each villain holding a piece of the control network. Squad assembles: Big Nate (muscle), Adam (tech), BZ (herbs = health), P-Mac (intel), Darrel (kennel), Deacon (infiltration).
*Objective: Learn controls, meet squad, receive Operation Recover briefing.*

**Prison District — Jeff City Ruins (Boss: The Warden)**
The Warden runs the control network's prison servers from the ruins. His Sec troopers patrol the cell blocks. Lonzo breaches the prison, frees an informant who reveals the brainwash signal routes through Red Light Row.
*Warden intro: "Prison District is MY cell block, Lonzo. You're just another inmate."*

## Act 2: The Network
**Red Light Row — St Louis River Neon (Boss: Neon Queen)**
Neon Queen broadcasts the brainwash signal through club holograms and ads. Her hired guns (the pink crew) harass Lonzo through the neon streets. Shut down the relays, confront her in the main club.
*Twist: One of her dancers slips Lonzo a data chip — someone inside wants the Queen gone.*

**Scrapyard — KC Yards (Boss: Rust Father)**
Rust Father melts down stolen Pulse Plet tech to build junk golems and rust hounds. His forge powers the villains' army production. Destroy the forges.
*BZ finds a herb strain growing in the toxic runoff — new heal item unlocked.*

**Herb Fields — Columbia Agri-Labs (Boss: The Chemist)**
The Chemist corrupted BZ's herb labs into a drug factory — this is where Beezy was drugged. Personal stake for BZ. Sabotage the labs, secure pure herb samples.
*Beezy (NPC) appears, guilt-ridden: "I didn't know what was on that drive, Lonzo. I swear." Lonzo: "Then help me fix it."*

**Kennel Row (Boss: Hound Master — Darrel's rival)**
The Hound Master mass-produces robotic pit bulls from stolen kennel tech. Darrel's keepers (his 2 bodyguard dogs with Lonzo tech) help breach the kennel. Free the captive animals.
*Darrel Law in action: big keepers fight beside you; small sell-dogs are freed, not fought.*

## Act 3: The Blade and the Signal
**Blade Alley (Boss: Blade Mother)**
Narrow alleys, blade cultists. The Blade Mother guards the approach to the Signal Tower — she's the Overlord's most loyal. Survive the gauntlet.
*Deacon's stealth section: optional silent path through the cult's back alleys.*

**Signal Tower (Sub-boss: The Archivist / Signal Breaker)**
P-Mac's counterpart. The Archivist controls the data streams brainwashing Carrie. Intense hack-battle: P-Mac vs Signal Breaker while Lonzo holds off waves.
*P-Mac: "His encryption is my old code... he learned from ME."*

**Easter Plains (Breather + Prep)**
Open plains, scattered settlements. Side missions, gear up, squad campfire scene. Characters reflect on how far they've come. Carrie appears in a dream/hallucination — first crack in the brainwashing.
*Carrie (static): "...Lonzo? Why do I know that name?"*

## Act 4: The Other Half
**Overlord Citadel (Final Boss: The Overlord)**
Black fortress, elite guard, every underling type. Fight up the tower. The Overlord reveals: he was Lonzo's former partner at Pulse Plet — he believes the brainwash "saved" Carrie from her trauma.
*Overlord: "You built an empire of tech, Lonzo. I just... took it."*

**Carrie Boss Fight**
Brainwashed Carrie in red armor, red helmet, blue core, blue eyes. She fights with telekinesis and pulse blasts. Mid-fight, she falters — Lonzo's voice breaks through.
*Carrie: "...why does my head hurt when you talk?"*

**Rescue Scene**
*Carrie: "I can hear them in my head... all of them... all the time."*
*Lonzo: "Then listen for me. I'm louder."*
*Carrie: "You came through all of Benway... for me?"*
*Lonzo: "I'd burn every district to the ground. You're the other half."*
