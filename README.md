**ACT 1 - Base Upgrade Only:** Only you + Carrie have base upgrade, nobody robotics. Father-in-law mad until happy.

**ACT 2 - Plain USB Theft:** Personal office computer plain USB no label. Leave BZ thinking small files, says "your wife's mind." Bad guys limited copy.

**ACT 3 - Red Alert - Taken:** WAIT! NOT SO FAST WE NEED TO HOLD TOGETHER! Carrie flies faster too late. Crowd regular wants help not realizing restraints because robotic brain. They take her. Everyone was regular until they take her.

**ACT 4 - Demanded Robotics + Wireless Upgrade:** Every main demands robotics but can't get AI level. Single wireless upgrade objective to track + assist - cars jets boats tasks across 300km². They think they have her but only half head. Half base secret. Small clues when she breaks free second now and again small conversation.

**ACT 5 - Near Death Sync:** You + father's near death allows other half to sync take back full control bigger scale disable enable anything. Revive disable cage power down weapons. We fight Overlord foreground, Carrie takes down hundreds background - more capable final.
